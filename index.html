THE PROBLEM

You're doing everything right. So why doesn't the advice work?

You've read the books. Tried the tips. Asked the internet at 2am. But the advice was written for a generic child. And yours isn't generic.

Every child is born with a unique temperament: their built in wiring that shapes how they react to change, why certain things overwhelm them, what calms them, and what sets them off.

When you understand their temperament, everything shifts. The meltdowns make sense. The strategies work.
